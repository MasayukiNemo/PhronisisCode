# TETTRIS — One T to Save a Pinch

No bombs. Just the right to turn the current piece into the most versatile T — and save a pinch by stacking.

## Four Novelties

### 1. Save Anywhere
S saves everything, L restores it forever. Auto save every 10 lines, Q restores the last checkpoint. Manual and auto never overwrite each other.

### 2. Two Hands
HOLD(C): swap one. T-POWER(F): at gauge 3, arm one T; press again to turn the piece into T. Separate from HOLD, so you can create a T on the spot. Every move: hold, T-POWER, or place.

### 3. Risk Only Charges
| Clear | Gain |
|---|---|
| 1-2 lines | +0 |
| 3 lines | +1 |
| 4 lines | +2 |
At 3, F to arm (gauge 0, stock 1); with stock, F turns the piece into T. If full and you clear 3/4 without arming, burst to 0. While holding stock, gauge recharges from 0 — stock 1 + gauge 3 is the hardest defense, sustainable with 1/2 clears. You don't charge by leveling; you charge by risking.

### 4. 3-2-1 Stance
LOAD(L/Q) and RESTART(R): always 3→2→1; unpause(P): only at Lv3+ 3→2→1. Gravity, lock and input are stopped during count.

## Controls
←→ move, ↓ soft, ↑/X clockwise, Z counter-clockwise, Space hard drop, C HOLD, F arm/fire (1st arm, 2nd fire)

## How to Play
1. SPACE to start → 2. aim for 3/4 to reach 3 and F to arm → 3. F to fire in a pinch → 4. S/L for practice, Q for insurance

Those who just stack, break. Those who stance, survive.
